### Analysis decisions the AI made on its own

These were not in the task brief and a group member should check them before
they go into the report:

- **Hypothesis (b) verdict.** The first version of `summarize_opt.py` judged (b)
  "supported" because `b4_float` drops from 2.77× (-O2) to 1.81× (-O0). The AI
  then looked at -O1 (2.54×, no vectoriser) and at clang's
  `-Rpass=loop-vectorize` report for `bench/b4_float.c`, found that the hot loop
  is not vectorised in either -O2 build, and changed the criterion so that (b)
  also requires a drop at -O1. The verdict became "not supported". The
  vectoriser check itself is not part of any committed script; the command was
  `clang-14 -O2 -g [-fsanitize=address -fno-omit-frame-pointer] -Rpass=loop-vectorize -Rpass-missed=loop-vectorize -Rpass-analysis=loop-vectorize -c bench/b4_float.c -o /dev/null`.
- **Thresholds.** "Clearly smaller" (10 %) and "markedly" (25 %) were chosen by
  the AI; the brief gave no numbers.
- **Different machine.** The sweep ran on my laptop, not on the machine used for
  the four main sessions. The AI recorded this in D20 and in the summary header
  and kept comparisons inside the sweep.
